# VIB strategy and creative brief

Status: foundation owner-approved under VIB-BRAND-FOUNDATION-001 (2026-11-24); artwork selection pending. Product candidate v0.0.0.19, [pinned source](https://github.com/vixttha/Version-Issues-Hub/tree/4fc9ac47462101b941ea2f78fc939d42a9de0032). Brand kit v0.0.1 is separate from product versioning.

## Product and audience

VIB is a standalone GitHub issue-version checkpoint and agent handoff toolkit. It gives humans and compatible agents a durable record of issue scope, candidate revisions, evidence and owner decisions. Primary audience: solo developers and teams using coding agents on GitHub. First decision: understand what VIB does and whether to inspect or install it. First surface: GitHub README, with compact repository avatar as secondary use.

## Desired character

Precise: aligned geometry and consistent widths. Durable: solid silhouette and deliberate joins. Independent: no resemblance to a required provider or coordination service. Human-controlled: a purposeful open endpoint, not a lock or shield. Professional: limited palette, no gradients, glow, generic AI brain, robot, or stock checkmark motif.

First size targets: 24–32 px symbol and 160–240 px lockup; these are proposed design targets, not verified minima. Make the mark usable in a single color before exploring accents.
