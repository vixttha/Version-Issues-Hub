# Naming

Use the [creative decision](../01-strategy/creative-decision.json) as the source for the current exact product and wordmark strings. Enter each spelling, case or punctuation change in its `names` object. Use this page for naming research, pronunciation, aliases, compatibility names and where each label belongs. Refer to the decision record when other kit pages need the exact strings.

For shortlisted names, record exact search terms, dates, relevant software or other collisions, and the decision. Note the target markets and uses. Record any owner approval with its scope. Keep clearance status distinct from creative selection.

Check [message hierarchy](../03-messaging/voice.md) and the [wordmark](../05-wordmark/wordmark.md) against the naming record.

Before drawing, record the owner's confirmation of the exact product name and wordmark text in the [creative decision](../01-strategy/creative-decision.json). A change in either field in `names` invalidates the previous confirmation until the owner confirms both current strings.
