# Slogan and voice

Write the slogan, a factual one-line descriptor, and the shortest useful overview. Identify the places where showing the slogan helps. Use product terms the audience already knows.

Describe how the brand should sound through two or three real examples, such as a README opener, an empty state, or an error message. Tie capability claims to current product evidence and record uncertain claims for review.

Use the [naming record](../02-naming/naming.md) for exact labels.
