# Wordmark

Use the exact lettering and case in the [creative decision](../01-strategy/creative-decision.json). Record proportions, optical spacing, and clear space here. Inspect individual letters and the complete outline. Specify logo-only, wordmark-only, and combined use. Record minimum usable sizes and the relative scale and gap for the combined layout.

Generate the wordmark PNG with @Images and verify each glyph by eye. Use real accessible text alongside or in place of the raster where the layout calls for reading or selecting the name.

Read the [naming record](../02-naming/naming.md) before changing lettering.
